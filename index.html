江味齋自 2025 年於 Edenburg 開業，秉持「用心做菜、講究食材」的信念，將台灣家傳的料理手藝，原汁原味呈現在南非的餐桌上。

從酥香排骨、滷得入味的控肉，到一碗熱騰騰的肉羹麵，我們相信，真正的好味道，來自於對細節的執著與對家鄉的思念。無論內用、外帶、外送，江味齋都希望成為你在異鄉最熟悉的那盞燈火。

Founded in 2025 in Edenburg, JiangWay is built on a simple belief — cook with heart, source with care — bringing the genuine flavours of Taiwanese home cooking to South African tables.

From crispy pork chops to slow-braised pork belly and a steaming bowl of pork thick soup noodles, we believe great taste comes from devotion to detail and longing for home. Dine in, take away, or have it delivered — JiangWay hopes to be your most familiar light away from home.

Yasungulwa ngo-2025 e-Edenburg, i-JiangWay yakhelwe enkolelweni elula — pheka ngenhliziyo — iletha ukunambitha kweqiniso kokupheka kwasekhaya kwaseTaiwan.

Kusukela ezinkomeni ezikhwabanisiwe kuya esobho lengulube, sikholelwa ukuthi ukunambitha okuhle kuvela ekuzinikeleni. Yidlela ngaphakathi, uyithathe, noma ilethwe — i-JiangWay ifuna ukuba ngukukhanya kwakho okujwayelekile.
