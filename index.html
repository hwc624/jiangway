江味齋坐落於約翰尼斯堡 Edenburg、Sandton，是一間僅設四桌的台灣家常小館。

「江」是奔流的河，是傳承的脈絡。「味」是記憶的根，是思念的形狀。「齋」是一方素淨之地，讓人靜下心來，好好吃一頓飯。

2025 年初，我們將台灣的便當文化帶到南非。每道菜都是一封家書——用滷汁、炊煙與米飯，寄給每一個離家在外的人。

JiangWay is a cozy Taiwanese home kitchen nestled in Edenburg, Sandton, Johannesburg — with just four tables and a whole lot of heart.

"Jiang" flows like a river, carrying tradition. "Wei" is the taste of memory. "Zhai" is a quiet place to sit, breathe, and eat well.

Since early 2025, we have brought Taiwan's bento culture to South Africa — one warm, home-cooked meal at a time.

IJiangWay iyindawo yokudla yaseTaiwan ekhona e-Edenburg, Sandton, eJohannesburg — enamathebula amane nje kuphela.

Kuze kusukela ngo-2025, siyiletha indlela ye-bento yaseTaiwan eMzansi Afrika — ukudla okulungiswe ekhaya ngesikhathi ngasinye.

Yilelo itafula elincane elingabalisa indaba enkulu — ngomfula, ngokhumbulo, nangokukhula.

Taiwanese Bento Box
主菜配白飯、炒青菜與小菜，簡單卻是最深的滋味。 Main protein with steamed rice, stir-fried greens and side dishes — simple, satisfying, soulful. Inyama enkulu kanye nezitsha ezikhethekile — elula kepha liyeneliseka.

滷肉飯
Braised Pork Rice
慢火滷製的五花肉，淋在白飯上，每口都是童年的記憶。 Slow-braised pork belly over steamed rice. The definitive taste of Taiwanese childhood. Inyama yokweluka echanywa phezu kwelayisi — ukudla okwenyuka umphefumulo.
🍜
牛肉麵
Beef Noodle Soup
清燉或紅燒，牛肉麵是台灣最具代表性的靈魂食物。 Clear broth or red-braised — Taiwan's most iconic soul food, bowl by comforting bowl. Isambulela esikhethekile senyama yenkomo — ukudla okwenyuka umphefumulo.
🥟
水餃
Steamed Dumplings
手工包製的水餃，皮薄餡飽，蘸醬吃最對味。 Hand-wrapped dumplings with thin skin and generous filling. Perfect with our house dipping sauce. Amadomu enziwe ngesandla — okumangalisayo ezindaweni zonke.

燙青菜
Blanched Greens
時令青菜現點現燙，淋上蒜油，清爽解膩。 Seasonal greens blanched to order, finished with garlic oil. Fresh, clean, essential. Imifino yezikhathi yodwa — imsulwa futhi yempilo.

台灣飲品
Taiwanese Drinks
從熱烏龍到冬瓜茶，每杯都是台灣街頭的記憶。 From hot oolong to winter melon tea — a sip of Taiwan's vibrant street culture. Iziphuzo zaseTaiwan — ngosizi nangoxolo.
